Starting My Journey: A New Beginning

March 2, 2026 Ana Alfaiate

Today marks the beginning of a new chapter in my life. After months of planning and thinking, I finally decided to create this space where I can share my thoughts, experiences, and reflections. This blog will be a place to document my progress, share stories, and connect with others who share similar interests.

Why start a blog?

I've always believed that sharing knowledge and experiences is one of the best ways to grow individually and as a community. Whether it's a technical deep dive into my research or a personal story about scouting, every post is a step towards better understanding myself and the world around me.

In this space, you'll find a mix of content:

  • Academic insights from my Master's journey at IST.
  • Technical tutorials and logic-based problem solving.
  • Reflections on community service and volunteering.
  • Stories from my travels and photography experiments.

I hope you find something here that inspires you or helps you in your own journey. Thank you for being part of this new beginning.
