Life Lessons from Scouting

February 15, 2026 Ana Alfaiate

My involvement with Scouting at Agrupamento 1066 has taught me invaluable lessons about leadership, teamwork, and resilience. This post reflects on the most impactful moments and how scouting continues to shape who I am.

The Law of Service

One of the core principles of scouting is to "be prepared" (Sempre Alerta). But being prepared isn't just about having the right gear for a hike; it's about being mentally and spiritually ready to help others in any circumstance. Through years of volunteer activities and campouts, I've learned that the most rewarding leadership is the one that serves the group.

I remember a particular rainy hike in Gerês where our team's morale was at an all-time low. It wasn't the technical skill of map reading that got us through, but the ability to listen, encourage, and distribute the load fairly among everyone. That experience taught me more about project management than any textbook ever could.

Scouting reminds me that we are part of something bigger. Whether it's building a temporary bridge or organizing a community event, the strength of the group always surpasses the individual. I carry these lessons with me into my academic and professional life ogni single day.
