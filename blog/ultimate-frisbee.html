The most fair-play sport in Portugal

March 18, 2026 Ana Alfaiate

(Personal Chronicle, Sports)

Coming from Denmark, I brought a wish with me… to continue playing a sport I didn't know before and loved playing in Lyngby, Copenhagen, at DTU - Introduction Week, DTU - Technical University of Denmark: Ultimate Frisbee. And you might be wondering… but what sport is this?! Let me explain…

In a few words, Ultimate Frisbee is a sport still not widely known, but with energy and different values. Different from any football or rugby. In Ultimate, there are no referees: the players themselves take responsibility for calling fouls and resolving conflicts. Thus, the "Spirit of the Game" (fair play) is the heart of Ultimate: it values respect, honesty, and communication above victory at any cost.

The goal is simple: get the disc to a teammate inside the final scoring zone (endzone) without dropping it. Whoever holds the disc cannot run… they are forced to pass, which makes the game fast and collective. When the disc falls, is intercepted, or goes out of bounds, possession automatically changes to the other team. I can say it's a mix between American football, rugby, and basketball.

Where to play in Portugal?

That was exactly my job when I returned from Erasmus. Here's what I found!

There is a Portuguese Association of Ultimate and Disc Sports (APUDD), with initiatives to expand the sport in schools. You can consult more information on the website www.apudd.pt.

Additionally, there are two clubs in the Greater Lisbon area. For the more attentive, you've surely seen some people running and throwing discs in Cidade Universitária, on the lawn in front of the Rectory of the University of Lisbon on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7 PM to 9 PM. This is the Disc’Over Lisboa club (Meeting point) with social media on Instagram and Facebook.

Ultimate Frisbee can also be played in a hall (indoor) and on sand (beach). There is another club for beach Ultimate Frisbee: Lisbon Ultimate Club (LUC), with a Facebook page and Instagram.

Teams across the country

Outside of Lisbon, there are still teams in various cities:

  • Abragacabra, Braga
  • Nortada, Porto
  • Gambozinos, Aveiro
  • Burning Ribbons, Coimbra
  • Leiria Flying Objects, Leiria
  • Lisbon Ultimate Club, Carcavelos
  • Disc’Over Lisbon, Lisboa
  • Vira’o’Disco, Setúbal
  • Javalis de São Luís, Odemira
  • Ultimate Frisbee Algarve, Portimão
