Why These Topics?

My research interests converge around the intersection of formal methods, computational logic, and biological systems. I'm fascinated by how computational approaches can unlock biological complexity, particularly in understanding gene regulatory networks through formal modeling and constraint satisfaction.

The choice of tools—ASP, SMT, ILP—reflects my belief that declarative approaches offer elegance and clarity in expressing complex problems. Rather than specifying how to solve a problem, we specify what the solution should satisfy, letting the solver find the answer.

Beyond computing, my interest in ophthalmology and optics stems from a deep commitment to healthcare innovation and understanding human vision—bridging technology with human wellbeing.
