Professor João Neves

Logic Programming & AI

★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Logic Programming
Spring 2024
Master's Program
Knowledgeable instructor with strong expertise in logic programming. The course material is challenging and intellectually stimulating. However, the pace can feel rushed, and supplementary explanations would benefit students new to the field.

Strengths & Growth Areas

  • Strengths: Deep technical knowledge, rigorous content, challenging problems
  • Growth Area: Could benefit from more scaffolding for beginners

Dr. Gabriela Ferreira

Thesis Advisor - Systems Biology

Master's Thesis Supervision
2024-2026
Research
An outstanding thesis advisor who provides invaluable guidance while fostering independence. She encourages creative thinking, provides constructive feedback, and supports student development both academically and professionally. Her mentorship extends beyond the thesis itself.

Why she's an excellent mentor

  • Genuinely invested in student success and growth
  • Provides detailed, actionable feedback on work
  • Encourages independent thinking and problem-solving
  • Open to trying new ideas and approaches
  • Excellent at connecting students with relevant resources
