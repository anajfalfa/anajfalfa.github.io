"Doubts are vitamins of faith - Pope Francis. We are the only Gospel people read."

Deep, honest, and thoughtful conversations about faith and its role in the contemporary world. A podcast dedicated to exploring Catholic doctrine, personal spiritual journeys, and the beauty of doubt as a catalyst for growth.

About the Project

"Vitaminas de Fé" (Vitamins of Faith) was born from the conviction that honest conversations about religion need a dedicated space. By interviewing people from different backgrounds and vocations, we aim to understand the authentic complexity of living a religious life today, without oversimplifying the deep questions that define our faith.
