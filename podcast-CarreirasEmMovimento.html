Carreiras em Movimento Alumni Podcast

A podcast initiative by Técnico Alumni, exploring professional journeys, challenges, and success stories of former students. Hosted by Ana Alfaiate, this project aims to inspire and connect the Alumni community through meaningful conversations.

Listen on Spotify Listen via Linktree LinkedIn Facebook Instagram

About the Project

The "Carreiras em Movimento" (Careers in Motion) podcast brings together Técnico graduates to share their experiences in the professional world, academia, and beyond. Whether you are a student exploring future paths or an experienced professional looking for new perspectives, these stories provide valuable insights into personal and career growth.
